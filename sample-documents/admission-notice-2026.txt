Admission Notice 2026

The admission form submission deadline for first-year BTech applicants is 15 July 2026.
Applicants must upload their Class 12 marksheet, transfer certificate, identity proof, and entrance exam scorecard during online registration.
The application fee for general category applicants is 1200 rupees and the application fee for reserved category applicants is 600 rupees.
Merit list publication is scheduled for 22 July 2026.
Document verification for selected applicants will be held from 25 July 2026 to 30 July 2026 in the main academic block.
